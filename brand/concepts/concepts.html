Cosecre-print — logo concepts

Same palette as the app (accent #4f8ef7 on ink #0b0d12). Shown full size and at the sizes that actually decide it: 64 px Dock, 32 px taskbar, 16 px title bar.

Concept 1

Batch

A wide fan of three sheets feeding one slot. Says “many documents, one printer” literally, and it is the most self-explanatory of the three. Cost: it is the busiest, and the fan is the first thing to go mushy when downsampled.

Aperture

Concept 3

Momentum

A document with a throughput badge — the base-object-plus-accent-badge pattern most utility apps use. Very legible small (white sheet, blue dot), but the chevrons say “fast”, not specifically “print”.

All three are pure vector with no hairlines, so they survive macOS downsampling the 1024 px master into an .icns and Windows into an .ico. The dark rounded tile is baked into the artwork, which is the usual choice for apps that aren’t Apple-designed. Switching the shipped mark to another concept is a one-file change — brand/icon.svg, then npm run brand.
